03 / QUEUE

Conversion rules

Supported inputs, rename logic, and the privacy model.

Native png · jpg/jpeg · gif · bmp · webp · avif · ico · svg — decoded directly by your browser (AVIF needs a 2020+ browser).
HEIC / HEIF .heic · .heif · .hif — iPhone photos & modern cameras, via the bundled libheif decoder. Multi-image containers are split into one WebP per image.
TIFF .tif · .tiff — via the bundled TIFF decoder; first page of multi-page files.
  • Rename rule: My Photo-1.png → My_Photo_1.webp — spaces & dashes become underscores, unsafe characters are sanitized.
  • Paths: copied as /uploads/Your_File.webp — edit the prefix in Output.
  • Privacy: there is no server. Files are read straight from disk into browser memory, converted locally, and held only until you clear the queue or close the tab. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or stored anywhere.
  • Offline: decoders are bundled in the vendor/ folder — no internet needed.
